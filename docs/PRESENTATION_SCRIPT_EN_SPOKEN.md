# ENGLISH — SPOKEN SCRIPT

## Slide 1 — Opening (15 sec)

Assalam-o-Alaikum, everyone.

Let me ask you something. You need an electrician today. Who do you call? A relative? A shopkeeper? You hope someone knows someone.

That is how millions of Pakistanis find workers every single day.

We fixed that. This is Digital Hiring — Rozgaar. Built by two students of NUST — me, [your name], and my teammate [name].

## Slide 2 — The Problem (15 sec)

Here is the problem.

Our workers are skilled. Painters. Plumbers. Electricians. Carpenters.

But they have no way to find work. And families have no way to find them.

No proof of skill. No payment protection. No trust between strangers.

## Slide 3 — What We Built (10 sec)

So we built one app that does the whole job.

In the next two minutes, I will show you everything inside it.

## Slide 4 — The AI (30 sec) — strongest moment

First — posting a job.

The employer does not fill ten forms. They just write one sentence:

"Need an electrician tomorrow morning in Lahore for wiring, thirty-five hundred a day."

Our AI reads that sentence and fills the whole form. Category. Skills. City. Wage. Date. Time. One click — the job is posted.

Then the matching engine starts. It checks skills. It checks the wage. It checks experience. And it checks distance — only workers within fifty kilometers of the job.

Every worker gets an offer that says WHY they matched. Ninety-two percent skill match. Two-point-three kilometers away. The worker sees the reason — and accepts with one tap.

## Slide 5 — The Money (25 sec)

Second — money. This is the part nobody else does.

When the worker accepts, the wage is locked in escrow. The money is held safely.

So the worker knows he WILL be paid.

When both sides mark the job complete, the money is released. If the employer cancels, the money goes back automatically.

And then both sides rate each other — one to five stars. A good worker builds a good name.

## Slide 6 — For the Workers (25 sec)

Third — our users are daily-wage workers. Many cannot read long menus.

So the app speaks. There is a Listen button that reads the screen out loud — in Urdu.

The whole app runs right-to-left in Urdu, with real Urdu fonts.

A worker can keep three professions on one account — a carpenter who also paints.

And anyone can work AND hire from the same account.

## Slide 7 — Everything Else (25 sec)

And there is more.

Workers upload photos of their past work. Employers see them before hiring.

There is in-app chat. There is an AI help assistant that answers in English and Urdu.

There is a live map to set your location.

The platform keeps searching for workers even overnight.

There is an admin panel where our team handles complaints and blocks scammers.

And the OTP — the login code — it works. Every time.

## Slide 8 — Is It Real? (15 sec)

Now — is this real? Yes.

It is live on the internet right now. It runs on a real database. Every change goes through automated testing. The full journey — job posted, offer accepted, payment released — is tested automatically before every release.

This is not a prototype. It is a product.

## Slides 9–10 — Closing (15 sec)

Pakistan does not lack skill. It lacks a system.

We built that system.

Digital Hiring — Rozgaar. Find workers. Find work.

It is live right now — you can try it tonight.

Thank you. Shukriya.
