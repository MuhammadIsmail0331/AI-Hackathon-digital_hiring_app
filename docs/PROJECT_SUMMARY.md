# Project Summary

**Paste-ready for the submission field "Project Summary" (1,050 characters — within the 200–1,500 limit):**

---

Digital Hiring (Rozgaar) is a mobile-first marketplace connecting Pakistan's daily-wage workers — painters, plumbers, electricians, carpenters — with households and businesses that need them, in English and Urdu with full right-to-left support.

Employers describe a job in one sentence; an AI assistant structures it into a complete posting, and the matching engine ranks rated professionals within a 50 km radius, explaining every score. Offers, escrow-secured wages, completion, and mutual star ratings form a full trust loop: a worker's pay is protected before work begins, and reputation compounds with every job. A dual-role model lets one account both work and hire, and multi-profession profiles fit real workers who master two trades. Fair Wage badges, background search for quiet hours, and an admin panel with issue tickets and user blocking keep the marketplace healthy.

Built with Next.js 15, TypeScript, PostgreSQL (Neon), Prisma and NextAuth by students of NUST for the Ali Baba AI Hackathon 2026.
